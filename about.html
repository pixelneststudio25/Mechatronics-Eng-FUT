About Us

Welcome to Mechatronics Engineering

The Department of Mechatronics Engineering at Federal University of Technology, Minna, is a centre of excellence in the interdisciplinary field of mechanical, electronics, computer, and control engineering. Our mission is to produce graduates with the skills to design and create intelligent systems and products that are smart and efficient.

Established in 2010, we have grown to become one of the most sought-after engineering departments in Nigeria, with state-of-the-art laboratories and a dedicated faculty.

Our Vision

To be a leading department in mechatronics education and research in Africa, fostering innovation and entrepreneurship.

Our Mission

To provide a conducive learning environment, engage in cutting-edge research, and produce graduates who can solve real-world problems through mechatronics.

Accreditations & Recognition

The department is fully accredited by the National Universities Commission (NUC) and the Council for the Regulation of Engineering in Nigeria (COREN). Our curriculum meets international standards.
