Academic Programs

Course Materials by Level

Access comprehensive course packs for each academic year. Click the download button to get a ZIP file containing lecture notes, assignments, and past questions.

100 Level - Foundation

The journey begins with foundational courses in mathematics, physics, and basic engineering principles. Students build the essential toolkit for understanding complex systems.

Core Courses:

  • MTH 101: General Mathematics I (Algebra & Trigonometry)
  • MTH 121: General Mathematics II (Calculus)
  • PHY 103: General Physics I (Mechanics & Properties of Matter)
  • PHY 126: General Physics II (Electricity & Magnetism)
  • CHM 101: General Chemistry I
  • GST 121: General Chemistry II
  • GST 121: Communication in English I
  • GST 112: Nigerian Peoples & Culture
  • STA 117: Staticstics
  • GST 121: Communication in English II
Download 100 Level Materials

200 Level - Core Engineering

Introduction to engineering sciences: mechanics of materials, circuit theory, programming, and thermodynamics. Students start applying theory to practical problems.

Core Courses:

  • EET 211: Engineering Mechanics I (Statics)
  • MEE 212: Engineering Mechanics II (Dynamics)
  • ELE 211: Electrical Engineering I (Circuit Analysis)
  • CPE 211: Computer Programming (C/C++)
  • MEE 213: Engineering Drawing & CAD
  • MEE 214: Thermodynamics I
  • MTH 211: Differential Equations
Download 200 Level Materials

300 Level - Mechatronics Core

Deep dive into mechatronics: sensors, actuators, microcontrollers, control systems, and embedded programming. Hands-on labs and mini-projects dominate this year.

Core Courses:

  • MEE 311: Sensors & Instrumentation
  • MEE 312: Actuators & Drives
  • ELE 311: Electronics I (Analog & Digital)
  • CPE 311: Microcontrollers & Embedded Systems
  • MEE 313: Control Systems I
  • MEE 314: Mechanics of Machines
  • MEE 315: Fluid Mechanics
Download 300 Level Materials

400 Level - Advanced & Specialization

Specialized topics: robotics, industrial automation, AI in mechatronics, and project management. Students undertake a major design project and prepare for industrial training.

Core Courses:

  • MEE 411: Robotics & Automation
  • MEE 412: Advanced Control Systems
  • MEE 413: Digital Signal Processing
  • MEE 414: Mechatronic System Design
  • MEE 415: Industrial Electronics & PLCs
  • MEE 416: Engineering Management & Ethics
  • MEE 417: Artificial Intelligence in Engineering
