Academic Programs

Undergraduate Program

B.Eng. Mechatronics Engineering

A five-year program covering mechanical, electrical, electronics, and software engineering with a focus on mechatronics. Hands-on projects, internships, and a final year project are integral.

Admission Requirements

  • Five O'Level credits including Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, English, and any other science subject.
  • UTME score meeting university cutoff.
  • Direct Entry: A-Level passes in relevant subjects.
